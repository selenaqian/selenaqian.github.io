Building Duke

Archives-focused research project looking at Duke University's founding

Video Intro Launch Project

I spent 5 hours a week in the archives looking through materials from the founding of the university, culminating in a video and interactive project looking at the places that inspired Duke's campus design. The interactive element is a StoryMap created with ESRI and ArcGIS, mapping the locations that Frank C. Brown and William P. Few visited while they were designing Duke and comparing those locations to Duke's campus.
